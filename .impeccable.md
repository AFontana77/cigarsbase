# CigarsBase — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Cigar enthusiasts — collectors, hobbyists, and daily smokers who want a real reference and a personal log. They browse after work, in the evening, or at a lounge. Phone on a wooden table, glass nearby. These are people who take their smoke seriously: they track vitolas, note wrappers, remember where they bought a box. Context is relaxed, deliberate, unhurried.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **refined, analog, serious.**

Not flashy. Not a startup. This feels like a curated spirits guide or a leather-bound tasting journal that someone actually uses, not a tech product. The site should make a cigar enthusiast feel at home — the same way a well-stocked humidor shop makes you slow down when you walk in.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Dark theme.** Cigar lounges are lit low. Evening use, warm glow, dark surfaces. A light theme would feel like a grocery store; this site needs the atmosphere of a dimly-lit back room.

**Font pair:**
- Display: `Bodoni Moda` — a high-contrast didone serif that reads like a vintage spirits label or a cigar band typography. Elegant, authoritative, old-world without being stuffy. NOT Playfair Display (too common). Bodoni Moda has that condensed elegance with dramatic thick/thin contrast.
- Body: `Mulish` — a clean, slightly geometric humanist sans that reads well at small sizes on dark backgrounds. Provides contrast with the display serif without competing with it.

**Color system (OKLCH, dark):**
- Background: `oklch(0.13 0.02 10)` — near-black with warm rose tint (not blue-black)
- Surface/card: `oklch(0.18 0.025 10)` — dark warm surface, clearly distinct from background
- Foreground: `oklch(0.93 0.015 10)` — cream/off-white with rose warmth
- Muted foreground: `oklch(0.58 0.015 10)` — mid-gray with warm tint
- Border: `oklch(0.25 0.02 10)` — subtle warm dark border
- Primary (crimson): `oklch(0.50 0.18 15)` — deep rose, readable on dark
- Primary light: `oklch(0.65 0.12 15)` — lighter crimson for hover states
- Gold accent: `oklch(0.78 0.10 70)` — warm gold for highlights and labels

**Reference feel:** The Cigar Aficionado print magazine layout (editorial column structure), aged whisky brand websites (dark, warm, confident), a Black Hawk Smoking Co. brand page. Anti-reference: generic app marketing sites with white backgrounds and blue CTAs, startup SaaS hero sections.

### Design Principles
1. **Atmosphere over information density.** The hero makes you feel like you're in a lounge, not reading a spec sheet.
2. **Editorial hierarchy.** Large display type contrasts with small body type. No small H2s on dark backgrounds where everything blurs together.
3. **Left-aligned, not centered.** Centered content feels like a brochure. Left-aligned feels like a magazine.
4. **Numbers are the design element.** "1,840 cigars" should be typographically prominent — that number IS the value proposition.
5. **No generic cards.** Features are presented as a clean numbered list or text-driven section, not icon-above-heading grid cards.

### Layout direction for homepage
- Hero: Left-aligned, two-column layout (text left, large stat number or visual right). Dark section with warm crimson accent. NOT centered-everything.
- Middle section: Bold typographic stat row (large numbers as visual anchors), then text content. Replace the 2×2 card grid.
- CTA section: Dark panel with high-contrast button. Clear and simple.
- Overall: Confident, unhurried, editorial.

### WCAG
Full WCAG 2.1 AA. All text on dark backgrounds must pass 4.5:1 contrast minimum. `oklch(0.93 0.015 10)` on `oklch(0.13 0.02 10)` is approximately 13:1 — well above requirement.
